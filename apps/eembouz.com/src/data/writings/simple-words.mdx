If you want to make progress faster, write concise explanations. Present ideas in simple words, clearly and directly, so others can challenge them, adapt them, rework them, or build upon them.

Concise explanations spread faster because they are easier to read and understand. The sooner an idea is understood, the sooner others can build on it and move forward.

Concise explanations speed up decision-making. They allow everyone to understand an idea and decide whether they agree with it.

Concise explanations are effective at every scale, from individual thinking to the progress of an organization, a community, or an entire civilization.
